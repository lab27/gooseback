Guests and events for our 2026 festival:

**Malin von Euler-Hogan:**

 filmmaker and actor based in Dublin, Ireland. Originally from North Carolina, she co-wrote and starred in the Vimeo Staff Pick short film *I Am In Control*, in addition to performing for over a decade on house teams at the Upright Citizens Brigade Theatre in New York and Los Angeles. ***Dead or Dying***, co-directed and co-written with John Purcell, is her first feature film.
