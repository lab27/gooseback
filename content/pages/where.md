The 4th Gåsebäck Film Festival takes place in 2 venues. \
Our film program will be shown at our partner cinema, [Röda Kvarn](https://www.biorodakvarn.se/), Karlsgatan 7, 252 24 Helsingborg.  

O﻿ur second venue is [G](https://gasebackkonstscen.se/)å[sebäck Konstscen](https://gasebackkonstscen.se/) - events held on-site will be announced soon.

For any enquiries just [email](mailto:info@gasebackfilmfestival.com) or message on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/gasebackfilmfestival/)!



**Access information**

The Röda Kvarn cinema offers the following: 

* a lift to the section floor if needed
* *hörslinga /* induction loop in both salons (for film content only)
* arm support on seats in the salons
* public parking with one accessible place directly outside the entrance
* an accessible toilet
* turning space for wheelchairs in the salons
